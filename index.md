# 3ball – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play **3ball** on iOS and Android, who collects it, and the choices you have.

**Developer:** 3ball
**Contact:** 3ballballball@proton.me

## The short version

- We don't run any servers and we don't collect your name, email or account details. 3ball works offline.
- 3ball shows ads from **Google AdMob**. AdMob collects some device information to show and measure ads. In the UK and EU, ads are only personalised if you agree.
- **Apple** or **Google** handles all payments. We never see your card or payment details.
- You can change your ad choices at any time in 3ball's **Settings → Privacy Settings**.

## Information collected by our partners

### Advertising: Google AdMob

When ads are shown, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

- Device and advertising identifiers (such as Apple's Advertising Identifier, Android's Advertising ID and App Set ID)
- Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city, not your exact location)
- How you interact with ads (for example, views and taps)
- Diagnostic and performance information (for example, app launch time and crashes)

This is used to show ads, measure how they perform, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud.

- **UK, EU and Switzerland:** when you first open 3ball, you're asked whether you consent to personalised ads. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.
- **iOS:** 3ball asks permission before using your Advertising Identifier for tracking. If you decline, it isn't used for tracking.

Learn more: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### Purchases: Apple App Store, Google Play and Unity

Optional in-game purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, such as which product it was and a transaction ID, so we can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. We never receive your payment details.

3ball uses **Unity In-App Purchasing** to communicate with the stores. Unity may process technical information about purchases and your device to provide this service. Learn more: [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)

### Information stored on your device

Your settings, game progress and purchase status are stored on your device so 3ball works offline. Uninstalling 3ball deletes this information.

### If you email us

If you contact us (for example, to report an ad or ask for support), we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

## Legal bases (UK and EU)

- **Consent:** personalised advertising. You can withdraw it at any time in **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
- **Legitimate interests:** non-personalised advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention.
- **Contract:** processing purchases you make.

## Your choices

- **Change ad consent:** in 3ball, go to **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
- **Limit tracking on iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Reset or delete your Advertising ID on Android:** Settings → Google → Ads (the wording varies by device).
- **Delete game data:** uninstall 3ball.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the UK and EU, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, or to object to or restrict how it's used. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google or Unity using the links above. We're happy to help: email us at [YOUR GAME EMAIL].

In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)).

## Children

3ball is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in 3ball are limited to age-appropriate content. If you believe a child has provided personal information through 3ball, contact us and we'll help get it removed.

## International transfers

Google and Unity may process information in countries outside the UK and EU, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
